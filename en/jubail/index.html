Our team in Jubail serves individual and corporate clients in the largest industrial city in the Kingdom, with practical experience in large industrial contracts and labor cases tied to factories and petrochemical facilities.

Jubail Industrial City is one of the largest planned industrial cities in the world, home to hundreds of factories and petrochemical facilities belonging to major companies like SABIC, Aramco, and their international partners. This massive industrial concentration creates a legal environment entirely different from any other Saudi city, where large industrial contracts and dense expatriate labor issues dominate the nature of the files we handle here. Jubail also sees continuous population growth given the thousands of workers and employees of various nationalities arriving annually to work at its diverse factories and facilities.

Most requested services in Jubail

Labor and employment cases top the list of requests in Jubail, given the huge number of expatriate and local workers at industrial facilities, closely followed by unfair dismissal and end-of-service claims. On the commercial side, we handle a large number of commercial debt collection files between subcontractors and main contractors, alongside commercial arbitration in large industrial contracting and supply contracts.

Given the city's nature, we also handle files specialized in the chemicals and petrochemicals sector and the industrial and manufacturing sector, from large supply contracts to quality-assurance and delivery disputes in industrial projects.

Courts and competent authorities

Jubail cases follow a judicial structure similar to the rest of the Eastern Province, with a very active Labor Court given the sheer size of the industrial workforce, and a Commercial Court for large industrial contract disputes, with comprehensive electronic tracking via the Najiz platform.

Examples of cases we handle in Jubail

Recurring scenarios include an expatriate worker at a petrochemical facility needing to understand their rights after a fixed-term contract ends, a subcontractor claiming overdue payments from a main contractor on an industrial expansion project, or a supply company facing a dispute over whether delivered materials matched agreed specifications. We handle each case with a plan that accounts for the industrial sector's scale and complexity.

Another recurring scenario: a local contracting company wins a subcontract on a large industrial project, then discovers the main contractor is delaying payments due despite the work being completed on the agreed schedule. We help the company document the actual work completed and formally claim payment from the main contractor, while assessing whether commercial arbitration is available if the original contract provides for it. We also regularly handle cases involving groups of workers of the same nationality facing the same issue at the same facility, helping them coordinate a unified group claim that saves time and cost.

Initial consultation vs. full representation in Jubail

In individual labor cases, a consultation and contract review is often enough before approaching the relevant labor authority. In large industrial disputes between contracting companies, full representation is necessary from the start given the significant financial value and complexity of these contracts. In many of these larger disputes, having a clear arbitration clause in the original contract significantly speeds up resolution compared to going directly to the Commercial Court.

When you actually need a lawyer in Jubail

Signs that call for immediate contact: receiving a termination letter without clear compensation, a subcontractor's payment delayed beyond the agreed period, or a dispute over whether supplied materials match technical specifications. Addressing these signals early protects your rights in a highly contractually complex industrial work environment. We also advise any worker or company facing pressure to sign a quick settlement without legal review to pause and contact us first, since some offered settlements may not reflect the actual value of statutory entitlements.

How working with us in Jubail works

It starts with a short WhatsApp message, followed by a quick assessment from a lawyer specializing in labor or industrial commercial matters, then a clear, time-bound plan. We pay particular attention to response speed specifically in expatriate labor cases given the sensitivity of the residency status tied to them. Where a file involves several workers at the same facility facing the same situation, we help coordinate unified follow-up that saves everyone time and effort.

Documents you may need before starting your file in Jubail

For labor cases, it helps to have a copy of the employment contract and the termination letter if one exists. For large industrial cases, having a copy of the contracting or supply agreement and the agreed technical specifications helps. We only ask for these documents once you actually move to filing a formal claim. In disputes specifically over the quality of supplied materials, any inspection or receiving reports issued by an independent party help speed up the file assessment.

Jubail and other Eastern Province cities

Our Jubail team coordinates with the teams in nearby Dammam and Qatif, given how interconnected the industrial supply chains are between these cities. Many companies operating in Jubail run their head offices from Dammam, and we ensure consistent handling of your file regardless of where it's officially registered.

Questions Jubail clients often ask before signing on

A common question: is a relatively small financial claim worth a formal lawsuit, or is direct negotiation with the other party enough? We help you assess this based on the other party's responsiveness and the actual amount in dispute. Another question common among subcontractors: can you claim overdue payments without harming the future working relationship with the main contractor? We explain the available options realistically for each specific case.

Why choose Afdal Mohami in Jubail

Our Jubail experience is built on deep practical understanding of the massive industrial work environment, from large supply contracts to expatriate labor cases — knowledge that qualifies our team to handle this sector's complexities rather than treating it like an ordinary labor case.

Our commitment to Jubail clients

We understand that many expatriate workers in Jubail worry about the effect on their residency status when claiming their rights, and that many small contractors hesitate to confront much larger main contractors. We commit to explaining your legal rights with complete clarity, without exaggeration in either direction, and help you decide what's right for your specific circumstances — whether that's pursuing a formal claim or seeking an amicable settlement.
