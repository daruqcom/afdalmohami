Our team in Sakaka provides legal advice and representation to individual and corporate clients in the capital of Al Jouf's agricultural region, with practical experience in agricultural and commercial cases tied to the northern region's distinct character.

Sakaka is the capital of Al Jouf region, known for its extensive olive farms considered among the largest olive groves in the world, alongside its strategic location near the Jordanian border. This specialized agricultural character alongside the border location creates a mix of agricultural inheritance cases and commercial cases tied to cross-border trade. Sakaka also includes important archaeological and historic sites, adding an extra dimension to some property ownership cases tied to land near these sites.

Examples of cases we handle in Sakaka
