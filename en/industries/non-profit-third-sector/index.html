Non-profit / Third Sector — Sector Legal Services

Specialist legal services for the Non-profit / Third Sector sector in Saudi Arabia.

We provide legal services for non-profit associations and foundations covering establishment and licensing, governance, and financial compliance, with practical understanding of this growing sector.

The non-profit sector is regulated by the National Center for the Development of the Non-profit Sector covering establishment, licensing, governance requirements, and financial transparency. We help associations and foundations meet these requirements and draft their bylaws.

Key Legal Challenges in This Sector

Among the most prominent challenges: establishing and licensing new associations and foundations, periodic governance and general assembly requirements, internal disputes among board members, and financial transparency and annual reporting requirements.

An Example of Files We Handle

Among recurring cases: founders planning to establish a new charitable association needing to prepare bylaws, or a dispute among board members of an existing association over a major administrative decision.

What You Should Know About Regulatory Compliance in This Sector

The National Center for the Development of the Non-profit Sector requires associations to submit a documented annual financial and performance report, and failure to comply with this report for two consecutive years may lead to temporary suspension of the association's licence.

A Practical Tip for Our Clients in This Sector

We recommend founders of new associations draft the bylaws with sufficient flexibility allowing future activity amendments without needing repeated modifications requiring new approvals each time.
