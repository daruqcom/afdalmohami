About Us

A nationwide law firm built to deliver the same service standard in every Saudi city — not one head office with lesser branches, but a single network at one level.

Afdal Mohami was founded on a simple idea: the quality of legal representation shouldn't depend on which city a client lives in. Instead of a single-office model serving one city, we built a network of licensed lawyers operating to the same procedures and the same follow-up standard across 24 Saudi cities.

How we work

Every file follows the same path: a quick initial consultation over WhatsApp, a clear assessment of the legal position, then a time-bound plan of action before any formal step. We track cases electronically via the Najiz platform and keep clients informed without requiring manual follow-up.

Our team

Our team includes lawyers and legal consultants licensed by the Ministry of Justice and the Bar Association, spanning practice areas including corporate & commercial, litigation, personal status, labor, criminal, and real estate, among others.

How We Work

Contact with us always starts with a brief WhatsApp message, followed by a quick assessment from a lawyer specialized in your consultation's area within hours, then a clear plan with defined duration and cost before any formal commitment. We strive for complete transparency at every step, from expected cost to a realistic timeline for your case.
