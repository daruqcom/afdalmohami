Employment Contracts Lawyers in Saudi Arabia

Expert employment contracts lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We draft and review employment contracts under Saudi law - offers, fixed and indefinite terms, executive packages, and the clauses that prevent disputes later.

The employment contract decides most later disputes: wage definition, probation, allowances, bonus discretion, notice, and non-compete scope. We draft contracts that comply with the Labor Law and Qiwa templates while protecting the party we act for.

We also build the surrounding framework - offer letters, policy manuals, confidentiality and IP assignment clauses, and executive agreements with equity or incentive components documented enforceably.

When you need this service

If you are hiring key people, signing an executive offer yourself, or your current templates were copied years ago from another company, review before signature costs a fraction of one dispute.

How we work with you

Send the draft or describe the role. We return a compliant, marked-up contract with plain-language notes on every material risk, aligned with Qiwa registration requirements.
