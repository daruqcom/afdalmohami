Real Estate & Construction Legal Services

Real Estate & Construction legal services in Saudi Arabia.

Afdal Mohami's Real Estate & Construction practice offers comprehensive legal services for everything related to property in Saudi Arabia, from simple rental disputes to large contracting agreements and public projects.

Whether you're a tenant facing a dispute with a property owner, a developer planning a new project, or a contractor needing a complex construction contract reviewed, this practice area is designed to cover all your legal needs related to real estate and construction in Saudi Arabia's growing market.

Key services within this practice area

We offer general real estate and property law, and rental disputes through the Ejar platform. For contractors and developers, we cover construction and engineering law and public projects and PPP. For buyers of properties under construction, we help with off-plan sales (Wafi) and real estate developer disputes.

Who actually needs these services

This practice area serves tenants and owners facing a disagreement over lease terms, buyers of residential units whose project delivery was delayed, contractors needing large contracting agreements reviewed before signing, and companies participating in public infrastructure projects through the public-private partnership system.

How working with us works

Contact starts with a brief description of your real estate situation, followed by an assessment from a specializing lawyer, then a plan defining the right path. In simple rental disputes, a formal notice through the Ejar platform is often enough; large contracting agreements need comprehensive legal review before signing.

Examples of cases we handle

Recurring scenarios include a tenant facing an owner's refusal to return a security deposit without clear reason, or a buyer who purchased an off-plan unit and didn't receive it on the agreed date. Another common scenario: a subcontractor claiming overdue payments from a main contractor on a large construction project.

Why choose Afdal Mohami for real estate and construction matters

We combine broad practical experience in simple rental disputes with deep knowledge of large contracting agreements and public projects, backed by up-to-date understanding of the modern Wafi and Ejar regulations governing Saudi Arabia's real estate market today.
