Title Deed & Land Disputes Lawyers in Saudi Arabia

Expert title deed & land disputes lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We litigate title deed and land disputes - ownership challenges, deed corrections, overlapping titles, and possession claims before the competent courts.

Land disputes in the Kingdom involve a specific evidentiary world: electronic and historical deeds, survey reports, and possession history. We handle deed authenticity challenges, boundary and overlap conflicts, and correction of deed data through the courts and Real Estate Registry procedures.

We also act in inheritance-driven land conflicts - undivided estates, unregistered historical transfers, and heirs discovering dispositions they never approved - where reconstructing the ownership chain is the core work.

When you need this service

If someone claims your land, your deed data is wrong or overlaps a neighbor's, or family land has sat undocumented across generations, these files reward early, methodical action.

How we work with you

We pull the full deed and survey history, commission the technical evidence courts rely on, and litigate or negotiate the correction - through to a registered, enforceable outcome.

Title disputes often need review of the underlying mortgage registered against the property, and we check both together before advising on next steps.
