Off-plan Sales Lawyers in Saudi Arabia

Expert off-plan sales lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We act on off-plan (Wafi) projects - buyer protection when projects stall, developer compliance with the off-plan sales regime, and escrow-related disputes.

Off-plan sales are regulated through the Wafi program: licensed projects, escrow accounts, and defined developer obligations. For buyers, we act when delivery dates slip, specifications change, or projects freeze - pursuing termination, refunds from the escrow structure, or completion claims.

For developers, we handle Wafi licensing compliance, sale agreement drafting that matches the regime, and dispute defense where delays have contractual and regulatory justifications to assert.

When you need this service

If your unit is seriously delayed, the developer is unresponsive, or you are a developer facing buyer claims and committee scrutiny, the regulatory framework defines the moves - use it precisely.

How we work with you

We audit the sale contract and the project's Wafi status, file with the competent committee or court with the escrow and licensing evidence, and pursue refund, compensation, or delivery orders.

Disputes with the developer over delivery or specifications are handled through our real estate developer disputes service, which coordinates directly with Wafi program matters.
