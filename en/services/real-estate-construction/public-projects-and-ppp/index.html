Public Projects & PPP Lawyers in Saudi Arabia

Expert public projects & ppp lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We advise on government contracts and PPP projects - tendering under Etimad, contract negotiation with public entities, and claims against government employers.

Contracting with the Saudi government runs through the Government Tenders and Procurement Law and the Etimad platform, with its own rules on qualification, bid integrity, variations, and penalties. We advise bidders and contractors across this lifecycle, including PPP structures under the Private Sector Participation framework.

When projects go wrong, we run claims against public employers - delay events caused by the authority, suspended payments, wrongful penalties and blacklisting - before the competent committees and the Board of Grievances.

When you need this service

If you are bidding for significant government work, negotiating a concession or PPP, or holding claims against a public entity that internal letters have not resolved, the procedural rules are your leverage - used correctly.

How we work with you

We review the tender or contract framework, position your bid or claim within the procurement law's mechanisms, and escalate unresolved claims through the formal dispute routes with full documentation.
