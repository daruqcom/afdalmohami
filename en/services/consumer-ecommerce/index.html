Consumer & E-commerce Legal Services

Consumer & E-commerce legal services in Saudi Arabia.

Afdal Mohami's Consumer & E-commerce practice offers legal services protecting the rights of consumers and traders in Saudi Arabia's growing market, from e-commerce disputes to hidden product defects.

With the huge growth of e-commerce in Saudi Arabia, the need for clear legal protection for both consumer and trader has grown. This practice area covers everything related to both parties' rights in commercial transactions, whether traditional or online.

Key services within this practice area

We offer general consumer protection for any commercial dispute affecting consumer rights, and e-commerce disputes for online purchases. We also help with product liability and hidden defects, and real estate fraud for those harmed by property fraud schemes.

Who actually needs these services

This practice area serves consumers who received a defective product or one different from what they ordered online, buyers who discovered a hidden defect in a product after purchase, real estate fraud victims seeking to recover their money, and traders needing to understand their statutory obligations toward customers.

How working with us works

Contact starts with a brief description of your situation as a consumer or trader, followed by an assessment from a specializing lawyer, then a plan defining the right path. In simple e-commerce disputes, a formal notice to the platform or trader is often enough to resolve the disagreement.

Examples of cases we handle

Recurring scenarios include a consumer who received a product completely different from what they ordered from an online store, with the trader refusing a refund, or a buyer who discovered a material hidden defect in a property or item after purchase. Another common scenario: a person who fell victim to a real estate fraud scheme needing to recover their money through legal proceedings.

Why choose Afdal Mohami for consumer and e-commerce matters

We combine practical understanding of the rapidly growing e-commerce landscape with precise knowledge of statutory consumer rights, backed by a commitment to helping both individual consumers and traders clearly understand their rights.
