Real Estate Fraud Lawyers in Saudi Arabia

Expert real estate fraud lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We pursue real estate fraud cases - selling one property to multiple buyers, fake unlicensed projects, and impersonating owners - recovering funds and criminally prosecuting perpetrators.

Real estate fraud takes multiple forms: selling the same property to more than one buyer, promoting fake or unlicensed real estate development projects through the Wafi program, and someone impersonating a property owner without authority to sell. We identify the type of incident and available evidence, building the criminal complaint file and civil claim together.

We pursue recovering paid funds through precautionary seizure of the fraudster's assets where possible, represent victims in criminal prosecution before the Public Prosecution, and coordinate between multiple victims of the same fraudster when it strengthens the criminal file's position.

When you need this service

If you discovered the property you bought was sold to more than one party, the project you invested in is fake or unlicensed, or you experienced owner impersonation, speed of action and evidence documentation determine your chances of recovering funds.

How we work with you

We gather purchase documents and financial transfers, file the criminal complaint with a precautionary seizure request, and pursue prosecution and the civil claim through to fund recovery or compensation.
