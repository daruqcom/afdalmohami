Trade Secrets Lawyers in Saudi Arabia

Expert trade secrets lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We protect trade secrets and confidential business information - NDAs that hold, internal controls, and action against misappropriation by employees and partners.

Trade secret protection is only as strong as your evidence of secrecy measures. We build the framework - confidentiality contracts, access controls, marked documents, exit procedures - that lets you enforce when information walks out the door.

When misappropriation happens, we move fast: preservation of evidence, cease-and-desist with teeth, and claims against the departing employee, the new employer, or the partner who used your data beyond the deal.

When you need this service

If a key employee left for a competitor with your pricing and clients, a negotiation counterparty is using your disclosed information, or you have no NDA framework at all, act before the information spreads further.

How we work with you

We assess what qualifies as a protectable secret and what evidence exists, secure the paper trail, then enforce through demand, negotiation, and litigation with damages quantified from the misuse.
