Intellectual Property Legal Services

Intellectual Property legal services in Saudi Arabia.

Afdal Mohami's Intellectual Property practice offers trademark, patent, and copyright protection services for individuals and companies in Saudi Arabia, from initial registration through anti-counterfeiting enforcement.

In an economy increasingly dependent on innovation and branding, protecting intellectual property becomes a necessary investment, not a legal luxury. This practice area covers everything a trademark owner, inventor, or creator needs to protect their rights in the Saudi market.

Key services within this practice area

We offer trademark registration to protect your business name or logo, and patents and industrial property for inventors and tech companies. For creative content owners, we offer copyright, and for companies facing counterfeit products, we offer anti-counterfeiting services. We also help protect trade secrets sensitive to a company's operations.

Who actually needs these services

This practice area serves new business owners wanting to protect their brand before official launch, tech companies that have developed an innovative product or solution, creative content owners like authors and designers, and companies that have discovered counterfeit products bearing their trademark in the market.

How working with us works

Contact starts with a brief description of what you want to protect, followed by an assessment from a lawyer specializing in intellectual property, then a plan defining the right registration type and expected timeline. Trademark registration typically takes several months through official authorities, and we follow the file with you step by step until the registration certificate is issued.

Examples of cases we handle

Recurring scenarios include an entrepreneur needing to register their trademark before launching a new product to avoid name impersonation by competitors, or a company discovering counterfeit products bearing its logo in local markets needing quick legal action. Another common scenario: a tech company that developed an algorithm or innovative product needing to understand how to legally protect this innovation.

Why choose Afdal Mohami for intellectual property matters

We combine practical experience in trademark registration across various sectors with technical knowledge of how to protect digital innovations, backed by particular response speed in counterfeiting cases that require urgent action to protect your brand's reputation.
