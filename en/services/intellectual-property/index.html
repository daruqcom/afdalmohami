Intellectual Property Legal Services

Intellectual Property legal services in Saudi Arabia.

Afdal Mohami's Intellectual Property practice offers trademark, patent, and copyright protection services for individuals and companies in Saudi Arabia, from initial registration through anti-counterfeiting enforcement.

In an economy increasingly dependent on innovation and branding, protecting intellectual property becomes a necessary investment, not a legal luxury. This practice area covers everything a trademark owner, inventor, or creator needs to protect their rights in the Saudi market.

Key services within this practice area

We offer trademark registration to protect your business name or logo, and patents and industrial property for inventors and tech companies. For creative content owners, we offer copyright, and for companies facing counterfeit products, we offer anti-counterfeiting services. We also help protect trade secrets sensitive to a company's operations.

Who actually needs these services

This practice area serves new business owners wanting to protect their brand before official launch, tech companies that have developed an innovative product or solution, creative content owners like authors and designers, and companies that have discovered counterfeit products bearing their trademark in the market.

How working with us works

Contact starts with a brief description of what you want to protect, followed by an assessment from a lawyer specializing in intellectual property, then a plan defining the right registration type and expected timeline. Trademark registration typically takes several months through official authorities, and we follow the file with you step by step until the registration certificate is issued.

Examples of cases we handle

Recurring scenarios include an entrepreneur needing to register their trademark before launching a new product to avoid name impersonation by competitors, or a company discovering counterfeit products bearing its logo in local markets needing quick legal action. Another common scenario: a tech company that developed an algorithm or innovative product needing to understand how to legally protect this innovation.

Why choose Afdal Mohami for intellectual property matters

We combine practical experience in trademark registration across various sectors with technical knowledge of how to protect digital innovations, backed by particular response speed in counterfeiting cases that require urgent action to protect your brand's reputation.

Quick Consultation vs. Extended Representation in IP Matters

Registering a new trademark or a simple copyright consultation is usually resolved with one consultation followed by routine registration procedures, while counterfeiting disputes and complex patent cases need extended representation involving specialized technical expertise. We determine the suitable route as soon as we understand the nature of your IP right and the type of dispute.

Documents That Speed Up IP Files

For trademark registration applications, having the final logo design and a precise description of the target activities and products helps. For counterfeiting disputes, samples of the original and counterfeit product and test-purchase evidence help build a strong file before the competent authority.

Sectors We Serve Within Intellectual Property

We serve clients from technology and media, esports and gaming, and retail, where trademark and digital content protection grows more important as the Kingdom's creative economy grows.

Questions Trademark Owners Ask

A common question: should the trademark be registered before launching the product or can it wait? We always recommend early registration, as any delay opens the door for a competitor to register a similar mark first. Another question: what should I do if I find a counterfeit product of my brand in an online store? We help you document evidence and file an immediate complaint to remove the counterfeit product before it spreads further.

How We Determine Fees for IP Cases

We offer clear fixed fees for routine registration services like trademarks and copyright, and hourly fees for complex disputes like counterfeiting cases and patents requiring additional technical expertise.

Common Mistakes We Help You Avoid

Among the most common mistakes: launching a new product or trademark without an initial check confirming it doesn't conflict with previously registered marks, potentially leading to a costly later dispute. Another mistake: not documenting ownership of creative content produced by freelancers through clear contracts, opening the door to future ownership disputes. We help you avoid these risks through proactive checking and review.

Our Commitment to Intellectual Property Clients

We commit to precisely tracking every registration or objection deadline, and give urgent priority to counterfeit content removal requests given how quickly damage spreads in today's digital environment.

Regional Trademark Protection

Many trademark owners ask: is registering in Saudi Arabia enough to protect my mark in other Gulf countries? No, protection is inherently territorial, and we help you coordinate additional registrations in GCC countries if you plan to expand outside Saudi Arabia, assessing country priority based on your actual business plan.

Building a Comprehensive IP Protection Strategy

We recommend business owners not settle for just registering the trademark, but build a comprehensive strategy covering all intellectual property assets: the mark, logo, any distinctive creative content, and related domain names. This comprehensive approach prevents gaps that competitors or counterfeiters might exploit later.

Protecting Trade Secrets Alongside Registered IP

Protecting your intellectual assets isn't limited to registered trademarks and patents alone, but extends to protecting trade secrets like customer lists and pricing strategies through tightly drafted confidentiality agreements. We help you build a comprehensive protection system covering both types of intellectual assets.

The Importance of Continuous Monitoring of the Counterfeit Market

Trademark protection doesn't end at registration; it needs continuous monitoring of online and physical markets to spot counterfeit products early. We help our clients establish a periodic monitoring system that detects counterfeiting in its early stages before it expands and becomes harder to address.

Handling Cross-Border IP Disputes

When the counterfeiter or competitor is outside the Kingdom, the case needs coordination with the other country's systems and sometimes international e-commerce platforms to remove infringing content. We handle this complexity through a network of relationships and practical experience dealing with these platforms.
