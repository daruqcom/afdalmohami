Education Lawyers in Saudi Arabia

Expert education law lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We provide legal advisory and representation for educational institutions, students, and parents - from private school licensing to disputes over education contracts.

The private education sector is regulated by the Ministry of Education covering licensing, tuition fees, and admission and dismissal conditions. We help private schools and universities meet licensing requirements, and draft enrollment contracts that protect the institution's and parents' rights alike.

We also handle tuition refund disputes, wrongful dismissal complaints for students, and teacher contract disputes, representing either side before education dispute resolution committees when needed.

When you need this service

If you are an educational institution facing a dispute with a parent over fees, or a parent seeking a fee refund or contesting a dismissal decision, the education sector's regulatory framework defines the correct path to resolution.

How we work with you

We review the education contract or school policy, identify the regulatory basis for the claim or defense, and represent you before the competent authority through to settlement.
