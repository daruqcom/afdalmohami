Alcohol & Intoxicants Offenses Lawyers in Saudi Arabia

Expert alcohol & intoxicants offenses lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We defend intoxicants cases - possession, consumption, manufacture, and distribution - with handling that accounts for these files' sensitivity and their particular impact on residents.

Intoxicants cases scale from consumption and personal possession to manufacturing and distribution, each with its own penalty. For expatriate residents there is an added dimension of major consequence: deportation that may attach to the judgment. We defend these files by disciplining the classification and contesting the seizure and testing evidence.

We review the legality of the arrest and search, the test results and their thresholds, dispute the distribution description where it rests on quantity alone without actual evidence of sale, and present mitigation and personal circumstances.

When you need this service

If you or a relative has been arrested in an intoxicants matter, make no detailed statements before consulting counsel - the difference between one classification and another can be the difference between a fine, prison, and deportation.

How we work with you

We obtain the seizure record and test results, build the defense on classification and procedure, and attend the investigation and trial while addressing the deportation question for residents within the strategy.

Frequently asked questions

Distribution requires evidence of sale or supply, not merely quantity. Contesting that description is among the strongest defense grounds because the penalties diverge sharply.

Deportation attaches to many judgments depending on the classification and circumstances. Within the defense we work every route that mitigates that consequence where possible.

Testing procedures and sample chain integrity are technically contestable, and any documented defect weakens reliance on the results.

Liability can arise from enabling or participation depending on the facts. Consult us before giving any statement if you were a peripheral party to an incident.
