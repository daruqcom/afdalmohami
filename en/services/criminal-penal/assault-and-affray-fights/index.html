Assault & Affray Lawyers in Saudi Arabia

Expert assault & affray lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We litigate assault, affray, and bodily harm cases - representing victims in proving the assault and claiming compensation, and defending the accused, particularly where self-defense applies.

Injury cases are built on the forensic medical report documenting the injuries, witness statements, and camera footage where it exists. For victims, we move fast to preserve this evidence before it disappears, file the complaint, and claim the private right for physical and moral harm.

For defendants, we contest attribution and the scope of the injuries, plead self-defense where the facts support it, and work the settlement and waiver tracks that end many of these cases with direct effect on the private right claim.

When you need this service

If you were assaulted, document your injuries with a medical report immediately - before anything else. If you are accused over a fight, do not rely on your verbal account; physical evidence decides these files.

How we work with you

We secure the evidence, medical reports, and witness statements, file the complaint or take over the defense, and run the trial and settlement tracks in parallel toward the best achievable outcome.

Assault cases sometimes carry a civil compensation angle alongside the criminal charge, and we pursue any personal injury compensation due to you in parallel with your defense.
