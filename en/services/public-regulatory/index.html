Public & Regulatory Legal Services

Public & Regulatory legal services in Saudi Arabia.

Afdal Mohami's Public & Regulatory practice offers legal services specialized in dealing with government and regulatory bodies in Saudi Arabia, from government contracts to immigration, residency, and professional licensing.

Dealing with government bodies requires precise understanding of administrative regulations that can differ from ordinary commercial rules. This practice area covers everything related to individuals' and companies' relationships with government and regulatory entities in the Kingdom.

Key services within this practice area

We offer general administrative and government law, and government contracts and public procurement for companies contracting with government entities. For those planning to reside in the Kingdom, we help with immigration and residency and nationality and premium residency. We also help licensed professionals with professional licensing and disciplinary matters.

Who actually needs these services

This practice area serves companies contracting with government entities through public tenders, individuals wanting to obtain premium residency or Saudi nationality, and licensed professionals facing a violation or needing to renew their professional license.

How working with us works

Contact starts with a brief description of your administrative or regulatory situation, followed by an assessment from a specializing lawyer, then a plan defining the correct path for dealing with the relevant government entity. We continuously track any new regulatory updates to ensure up-to-date advice.

Examples of cases we handle

Recurring scenarios include a company participating in a government tender needing to review contract terms before submission, or an individual wanting to apply for the premium residency program needing to understand the requirements. Another common scenario: a professional facing a violation from a regulatory authority needing the appropriate legal response.

Why choose Afdal Mohami for public and regulatory matters

We combine up-to-date knowledge of constantly evolving government regulations with practical experience dealing with various regulatory bodies, backed by a commitment to tracking any new updates affecting your legal situation.

Quick Consultation vs. Extended Representation in Administrative & Regulatory Matters

A consultation about renewing a simple professional licence or a municipal procedure inquiry is usually resolved with one consultation, while major administrative appeals before the Board of Grievances and government contract disputes need extended representation involving several hearings and technical memoranda. We determine the suitable route as soon as we assess the nature of your decision or dispute.

Documents That Speed Up Administrative & Regulatory Cases

For appeals against administrative decisions, having a copy of the disputed decision and the date of learning of it helps. For government contract disputes, a copy of the contract and execution correspondence with the government entity help speed up accurate case assessment before the short statutory deadlines expire.

Sectors We Serve Within Administrative & Regulatory

We serve clients from government contracting and the non-profit sector, in addition to individuals facing administrative decisions affecting their legal or employment status.

Questions Individuals and Companies Ask in Administrative Cases

A common question: can enforcement of a harmful administrative decision be suspended before the appeal is decided? Yes, through an urgent suspension request we file with the original claim if its conditions are met. Another question from contractors: what do I do if a government entity forfeits a bank guarantee without clear justification? We help you contest the unjustified or excessive forfeiture before the competent authority.

How We Determine Fees for Administrative & Regulatory Cases

We offer fixed fees for simple consultations and appeals, and fees depending on dispute scale in major government contract cases and complex appeals before the Board of Grievances.

Common Mistakes We Help You Avoid

Among the most common mistakes: missing the short deadline to appeal an administrative decision due to not knowing the right to object exists at all. Another common mistake in government contracts: bidding on a tender without reviewing the tender documents carefully enough, potentially leading to disqualification for simple formal reasons that could have been avoided. We help you pay attention to all these precise details.

Our Commitment to Administrative & Regulatory Clients

We commit to precisely tracking short statutory deadlines, and give urgent priority to suspension requests that may protect our clients' rights from immediate, otherwise irreparable harm.

Administrative Cases for Foreign Investors and Companies

Many foreign investors ask: does the administrative appeal route differ if the company is foreign-owned? No, appeal procedures before the Board of Grievances are unified for all parties, and we help foreign investors understand these procedures in clear language accounting for their lack of complete familiarity with the Saudi judicial system.

The Importance of Proactive Licensing and Compliance Follow-up

We always recommend companies periodically review their licensing status and regulatory compliance, rather than waiting for a violation notice or alert from the competent government entity. This proactive follow-up saves you fines and penalties that could have easily been avoided through simple periodic review.

Handling Regulatory Decisions Issued by Multiple Authorities

Clients sometimes face conflicting decisions issued by different government entities concerning the same activity, such as a conflict between a municipal licence and another sector authority's approval. We help you understand the correct legal priority between these decisions, and follow up coordination with the relevant authorities to reach a practical solution removing this conflict.

The Importance of Practical Understanding of How Government Entities Work

Effective dealing with government entities requires practical understanding of how their internal work actually proceeds, not just abstract knowledge of the regulatory text. We have accumulated practical experience dealing with various government entities, helping us anticipate procedural challenges and address them before they become a real obstacle to your file.

Handling Urgent Administrative Decisions

Some administrative decisions require immediate action within hours, not days, such as travel ban decisions or account freezes linked to an administrative procedure. We handle these urgent cases with utmost priority, recognizing that delay may mean permanently losing the right.
