Public & Regulatory Legal Services

Public & Regulatory legal services in Saudi Arabia.

Afdal Mohami's Public & Regulatory practice offers legal services specialized in dealing with government and regulatory bodies in Saudi Arabia, from government contracts to immigration, residency, and professional licensing.

Dealing with government bodies requires precise understanding of administrative regulations that can differ from ordinary commercial rules. This practice area covers everything related to individuals' and companies' relationships with government and regulatory entities in the Kingdom.

Key services within this practice area

We offer general administrative and government law, and government contracts and public procurement for companies contracting with government entities. For those planning to reside in the Kingdom, we help with immigration and residency and nationality and premium residency. We also help licensed professionals with professional licensing and disciplinary matters.

Who actually needs these services

This practice area serves companies contracting with government entities through public tenders, individuals wanting to obtain premium residency or Saudi nationality, and licensed professionals facing a violation or needing to renew their professional license.

How working with us works

Contact starts with a brief description of your administrative or regulatory situation, followed by an assessment from a specializing lawyer, then a plan defining the correct path for dealing with the relevant government entity. We continuously track any new regulatory updates to ensure up-to-date advice.

Examples of cases we handle

Recurring scenarios include a company participating in a government tender needing to review contract terms before submission, or an individual wanting to apply for the premium residency program needing to understand the requirements. Another common scenario: a professional facing a violation from a regulatory authority needing the appropriate legal response.

Why choose Afdal Mohami for public and regulatory matters

We combine up-to-date knowledge of constantly evolving government regulations with practical experience dealing with various regulatory bodies, backed by a commitment to tracking any new updates affecting your legal situation.
