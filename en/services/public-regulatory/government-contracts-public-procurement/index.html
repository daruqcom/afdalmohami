Government Contracts & Public Procurement Lawyers in Saudi Arabia

Expert government contracts & public procurement lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We provide advisory and representation for companies contracting with government entities through the Etimad platform - qualification, tenders, and disputes arising from government contracts.

Contracting with government entities is governed by the Government Tenders and Procurement Law with precise requirements for qualification, bid submission, and performance guarantees. We help companies meet qualification requirements via the Etimad platform, and review tender documents before submission to avoid disqualification on formal grounds.

When a dispute arises with a government entity, we represent the contractor in claims for time extension and compensation for unjustified delay by the entity, and pursue objections against disqualification or forfeiture decisions before the dispute review committees and then the Board of Grievances when needed.

When you need this service

If you are submitting a bid for a major government tender, or facing a dispute with a government entity over an existing contract's execution, familiarity with the procurement law determines the strength of your negotiating and legal position.

How we work with you

We review the tender documents or existing contract, identify the risk points and opportunities, and represent you in preparing the bid or the dispute before the competent entity or committee through to final resolution.
