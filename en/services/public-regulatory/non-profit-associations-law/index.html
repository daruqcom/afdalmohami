Non-profit & Associations Lawyers in Saudi Arabia

Expert non-profit & associations law lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We provide legal advisory for non-profit associations and foundations - establishment and licensing before the National Center for the Development of the Non-profit Sector, governance, and ongoing compliance.

Establishing an association or non-profit foundation requires meeting precise conditions before the National Center for the Development of the Non-profit Sector, from the bylaws to the required administrative structure. We help you prepare the complete establishment file and draft bylaws consistent with the regulations governing the sector.

We also handle ongoing governance requirements after establishment: general assemblies, annual performance reports, and compliance with financial transparency rules, representing associations in any internal dispute between members or the board.

When you need this service

If you are planning to establish an association or non-profit foundation, or manage an existing entity needing governance review or facing an internal dispute, familiarity with non-profit sector regulations protects the association's continuity.

How we work with you

We review the establishment purpose and proposed structure, prepare the establishment file and bylaws, and follow the application before the National Center through to licensing or resolving the existing dispute.

Non-profit governance disputes among board members are handled with the same care as our shareholder disputes practice, adapted to the association bylaws.
