Legal Memoranda & Pleadings Drafting in Saudi Arabia

Expert legal memoranda & pleadings drafting lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We draft the memoranda and pleadings that win cases - statements of claim, defenses, appeal memoranda, and Supreme Court petitions in precise legal Arabic.

Saudi judges decide largely on the written file. A memorandum that states the facts cleanly, anchors every request in nizam and precedent, and answers the other side's strongest point is worth more than any number of hearings. Drafting is our core craft.

We draft for direct clients and for other lawyers and in-house teams who want specialist written advocacy on a specific filing - from first-instance claims to appeal and objection memoranda with tight deadlines.

When you need this service

If a critical filing is due - a defense, an appeal against a bad judgment, an objection before the Supreme Court - the quality of that single document often decides the outcome.

How we work with you

Send the case file and deadline. We return a structured draft grounded in the record and the applicable regulations, revise it with your input, and deliver it filing-ready before the deadline.

Well-drafted memoranda matter most once a case reaches litigation or arbitration, and we prepare them as part of the same file, not as a separate disconnected service.
