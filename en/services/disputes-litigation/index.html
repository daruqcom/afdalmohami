Disputes & Litigation Legal Services

The directory connects you with lawyers for Disputes & Litigation matters.

Afdal Mohami Directory is a marketing platform that introduces you to independent lawyers. It is not a law firm and does not provide legal services.

For Disputes & Litigation matters, the directory points you to lawyers who specialise in representation before Saudi Arabia's various levels of litigation, from the first hearing through the enforcement stage, whether through traditional courts, arbitration, or mediation.

When a dispute reaches a point where direct negotiation can't resolve it, legal representation before the relevant authority becomes necessary to protect your rights. This practice area covers every dispute-resolution path available in the Saudi system, from traditional litigation to faster alternatives like arbitration and mediation.

Looking for Disputes & Litigation Legal Services? This lawyer directory lists independent lawyers who handle this area and can put you in touch with one in Riyadh, Jeddah or Dammam. Browse the full lawyer directory or compare other legal services in Saudi Arabia.

Key services within this practice area

The lawyers offer litigation and advocacy for full representation before courts at various levels, and arbitration as a faster alternative for large commercial disputes whose contracts include an arbitration clause. For those who prefer an amicable solution, the lawyers offer mediation and dispute resolution. After a ruling is issued, they handle execution court and judgment enforcement to help you receive your rights, alongside electronic litigation through the Ministry of Justice's modern systems.

Who actually needs these services

This practice area serves individuals and companies who've exhausted direct negotiation options with the other party, whether they're plaintiffs claiming a right or defendants needing to defend their position. Some clients come to the directory after trying to resolve a dispute amicably without success, while others prefer to start with arbitration directly if their commercial contract specifies it.

How it works

Contact starts with a brief description of your dispute, followed by an honest assessment of how strong your legal position is from a lawyer specializing in litigation, then a plan outlining the right path (litigation, arbitration, or mediation) and expected timeline for each option. The lawyers tell you honestly how strong a case is before you proceed.

Examples of cases

Recurring scenarios include a company claiming overdue dues from a commercial party who refused payment despite repeated amicable requests, or a large commercial dispute whose original contract specifies arbitration as a mandatory path for resolving disagreements. Another common scenario: a client who won a court ruling but faces difficulty with actual enforcement, needing the file followed up before the execution court.
