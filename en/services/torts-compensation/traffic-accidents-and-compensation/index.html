Traffic Accidents & Compensation Lawyers in Saudi Arabia

Expert traffic accidents & compensation lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We represent traffic accident victims in claiming full compensation due from insurance companies and the at-fault party - from diyah to material and moral damages.

The traffic report is the foundation of any compensation claim, and the fault percentage recorded in it determines the entire course of the case. We review the report immediately after the accident, contest the fault percentage when inaccurate, and build the claim file on documented medical reports for injuries and invoices for material damages.

We pursue the claim with the insurance company first, and when it refuses or underpays, we move to a judicial claim or before the insurance dispute resolution committees, claiming diyah and arsh in death or severe injury cases alongside civil compensation.

When you need this service

If you were in a traffic accident and feel the compensation offered by insurance does not cover your actual damages, or need help interpreting the traffic report and fault percentage, early action preserves your right before evidence is lost or deadlines expire.

How we work with you

We gather the traffic report, medical reports, and damage invoices, prepare the full claim, and pursue it with the insurance company or before the competent judicial authority until you receive the compensation due.
