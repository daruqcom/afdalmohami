Diyah & Blood Money & Arsh Lawyers in Saudi Arabia

Expert diyah & blood money & arsh lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We pursue Sharia diyah and arsh claims in death accidents and bodily injuries - from determining the correct amount to actually collecting it from the liable party.

Diyah and arsh are Sharia amounts prescribed by law that vary based on the type and circumstances of the injury or death: full diyah, a percentage of it, or arsh for a specific wound or limb according to a precise Sharia schedule. We precisely determine the correct amount due for your case, as many claims are settled for less than the actual right without this determination.

We pursue collecting diyah or arsh directly from the liable party or through insurance if available, resorting to compulsory enforcement procedures when the liable party refuses to pay, coordinating between the diyah claim and additional civil compensation when both are due.

When you need this service

If you are heirs of someone deceased in an incident deserving diyah, or injured with a specific Sharia-determined arsh, correctly determining the amount from the start prevents settling the file for less than your actual right.

How we work with you

We determine the type of injury or death and the amount of diyah or arsh due under Sharia, claim it from the liable party or insurance, and pursue collection through to actual receipt.

Diyah claims frequently arise from the same incidents handled under our traffic accident compensation service, and we pursue both amounts within one claim.
