Finance, Banking & Tax Legal Services

Finance, Banking & Tax legal services in Saudi Arabia.

Afdal Mohami's Finance, Banking & Tax practice offers specialized legal services covering everything related to financial, banking, and tax dealings for individuals and companies in Saudi Arabia, from debt collection to tax compliance.

Financial cases differ from other legal specialties in that they often require fast action, particularly with bounced cheques or defaulted debts whose value erodes over time. This practice area covers the full spectrum of legal financial needs, from simple collection to complex tax compliance.

Key services within this practice area

We offer banking and finance law for companies dealing with banking entities, and debt collection for outstanding financial claims, alongside financial claims and bounced cheques. For companies in the emerging financial sector, we offer fintech law, and for all companies we help with tax and zakat compliance with the requirements of the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority.

Who actually needs these services

This practice area serves traders facing bounced cheques from their customers, and companies needing to review their tax and zakat obligations before filing deadlines, in addition to fintech startups needing to understand their specific regulatory framework. Speed matters particularly in collection cases, since the chances of recovering the amount decrease the longer the delay.

How working with us works

Contact starts with a brief description of your financial situation, followed by a quick assessment from a specializing lawyer, then a plan defining the right path (formal notice, collection lawsuit, or compliance review) and expected timeline. In bounced-cheque cases specifically, we move quickly given the potential criminal nature of this type of violation.

Examples of cases we handle

Recurring scenarios include a trader receiving a bounced cheque from a customer needing a quick claim before the situation worsens, or a company needing to review its zakat obligations before the filing deadline. Another common scenario: a fintech startup needing to understand licensing requirements from the Saudi Central Bank before launching its product.

Why choose Afdal Mohami for finance, banking, and tax matters

We combine the response speed necessary for collection cases with up-to-date knowledge of the constantly evolving Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority regulations, backed by practical understanding of the emerging financial sector's needs in Saudi Arabia.

Quick Consultation vs. Extended Representation in Financial Matters

Matters like collecting a bounced cheque or a simple tax inquiry are often resolved with a single consultation or demand memorandum, while bankruptcy cases, major tax objections, and money laundering investigations need extended representation before specialized committees. We clarify from the first consultation which route fits the nature of your file.

Documents That Speed Up Financial Files

For collection cases, having the cheque or executive instrument and correspondence record with the debtor helps. For tax cases, having the ZATCA assessment and relevant accounting records helps. For insurance disputes, the insurance policy and reasons for claim rejection speed up the initial assessment.

Sectors We Serve Within Finance, Banking & Tax

We serve clients from banking services, insurance, and the digital economy, including fintech startups needing precise understanding of SAMA and CMA regulations.

Questions Our Clients Ask in Financial Cases

A common question: can a bounced cheque be collected quickly without needing a full lawsuit? Yes, a cheque is an executive instrument in many cases and compulsory enforcement procedures can be pursued directly. Another question: is objecting to a tax assessment always worth the effort? We recommend carefully reviewing every assessment, as many contain calculation or classification errors correctable simply through a proper objection.

How We Determine Fees for Financial Cases

Fees vary by case type: fixed fees for simple collection requests, hourly fees for complex tax advisory, with the possibility of linking part of the fee to actual collection amount in major commercial debt cases.

Common Mistakes We Help You Avoid

Among the most common mistakes: ignoring the short deadline to object to a tax assessment, permanently losing the right to appeal. Another common mistake: relying on improperly documented collection paper as an executive instrument, slowing collection procedures later. We help you avoid these pitfalls through precise review of every document before taking any formal action.

Our Commitment to Finance, Banking & Tax Clients

We commit to rapid action in collection cases given their direct impact on our clients' cash flow, and give particular priority to short statutory deadlines in tax and financial objections to ensure no right is lost through delay.

Cross-City Financial Cases

Many clients with debtors in different cities ask: does the debtor's location affect collection speed? No, compulsory enforcement procedures through the Najiz platform are unified Kingdom-wide, and we follow your file with the same efficiency regardless of the other party's location or the court competent to hear the enforcement request.

The Importance of Prior Financial Documentation

We always recommend companies and individuals keep precise documentation of every important financial transaction: signed contracts, official invoices, and written correspondence instead of verbal agreements. This prior documentation transforms any future financial dispute from a complex evidentiary battle into a direct claim supported by clear evidence, significantly speeding up the collection or settlement process.

Handling Complex Banking Disputes

Bank disputes, such as unjustified account freezes or refusal to execute a financial transfer, need special understanding of SAMA regulations alongside general regulations. We handle this type of dispute through the correct channels, whether directly with the bank or through the competent banking disputes committee, ensuring the fastest possible resolution for our clients.

The Importance of Accounting Understanding Alongside Legal Understanding

Many financial and tax cases require precise accounting understanding alongside pure legal understanding. We collaborate with trusted accountants and financial auditors when needed, ensuring our legal strategy is built on correct understanding of the actual numbers and accounting entries in your file.

Handling Investors and Startups in the Financial Sector

We notice growing numbers of startups operating in fintech, needing special understanding of the intersection between SAMA and CMA regulations and their innovative business model. We help these companies position themselves correctly from the early formation stage.
