Visitation Enforcement Lawyers in Saudi Arabia

Expert visitation enforcement lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We pursue visitation arrangement cases and their compulsory enforcement when the custodian refuses, protecting the non-custodial parent's right to stay in contact with their children.

The right to visitation is guaranteed for the non-custodial parent, and the court sets it out in a clear schedule of days, times, and handover location. When the custodian refuses to implement this schedule, we pursue the compulsory enforcement mechanisms the law provides to ensure the visitation right is respected in practice, not just on paper.

We also handle requests to modify the visitation schedule as either party's circumstances change, and cases to prevent traveling with children without the other party's permission, documenting every instance of refusal to support the enforcement request or even reconsideration of custody itself where refusal is repeated.

When you need this service

If the custodian is preventing you from seeing your children according to the set schedule, or you need to modify a visitation schedule that no longer fits your circumstances, continuous documentation of every refusal incident strengthens your position before the court.

How we work with you

We review the existing visitation arrangement instrument and documented refusal incidents, prepare the compulsory enforcement request or schedule modification, and follow through to actual enforcement before the competent authority.
