Family & Personal Status Legal Services

Family & Personal Status legal services in Saudi Arabia.

Afdal Mohami's Family & Personal Status practice offers legal services attentive to the sensitive human side of family matters in Saudi Arabia, from divorce and custody to domestic violence protection and marriage contracts.

Personal status cases differ from any other legal specialty in that they touch a person's closest human relationships, requiring a lawyer who combines legal firmness with human sensitivity at once. This practice area covers every stage of the legal family relationship, from the marriage contract through settling the effects of separation if it happens.

Key services within this practice area

We offer divorce and khula and custody and alimony for parties going through separation. For more sensitive cases, we offer domestic violence protection, and for those planning to start a marital relationship, we help with marriage contracts and prenups and marriage dissolution and paternity.

Who actually needs these services

This practice area serves spouses facing a disagreement difficult to resolve amicably, parents needing a clear custody arrangement after separation, domestic violence victims needing urgent legal protection, and those wanting to document their rights before marriage through a clear agreement.

How working with us works

Contact starts with a calm, confidential description of your situation, followed by a consultation from a lawyer specializing in family cases who's mindful of the situation's sensitivity, then a plan that accounts for all parties' interests, especially children if any exist. We always prefer exploring amicable solutions first when possible and in everyone's interest.

Examples of cases we handle

Recurring scenarios include a wife requesting khula after marital life becomes untenable, or a dispute between parents over custody arrangements after divorce. Another common scenario: a person needing urgent legal protection from domestic violence who needs to move quickly to ensure their safety.

Why choose Afdal Mohami for personal status matters

We combine the legal firmness necessary to protect your rights with human sensitivity toward the difficult emotional side of these cases, always preferring solutions that preserve children's stability and best interests.
