Marriage Contracts & Prenup Lawyers in Saudi Arabia

Expert marriage contracts & prenup lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We draft marriage contracts and accompanying agreements that protect both parties' rights, including special wife's conditions and financial estate arrangements.

A marriage contract under Saudi law can include special conditions for the wife that become binding if they do not contradict the contract's purpose - such as a condition to continue working or studying, or specifying the place of residence. We draft these conditions precisely to ensure their later enforceability in court.

We also prepare separate premarital financial estate agreements between spouses, particularly in cases involving existing assets or businesses, protecting each party in the event of any future change in the relationship.

When you need this service

If you are about to marry and want to document special conditions, or you own business assets you want to protect within a clear arrangement before marriage, prior legal drafting prevents potential future disputes.

How we work with you

We listen to your wishes and financial circumstances, draft the conditions and agreements in enforceable legal language, and document them within the marriage contract or as a separate certified addendum.

A prenuptial agreement is often paired with broader family wealth planning, especially when one party holds significant business assets.
