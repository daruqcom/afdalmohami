Commercial Contracts & Agreements Lawyers in Saudi Arabia

Expert commercial contracts & agreements lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We draft, review, and negotiate commercial contracts under Saudi law - supply, services, distribution, NDAs, and framework agreements that hold up when tested.

A contract is only as good as its enforceability before a Saudi court or arbitral tribunal. We draft agreements with clear scope, payment triggers, liability caps, termination mechanics, and dispute clauses that fit how Saudi commercial courts actually read them.

We also review counterparty paper before you sign, flag the clauses that shift risk onto you, and negotiate amendments. For companies with repeat transactions, we build template banks so your team signs faster with less risk.

When you need this service

If you are about to sign a significant supply, services, or partnership agreement, if a counterparty sent you their standard terms, or if your existing templates were written for another jurisdiction, have them reviewed before signature - not after a dispute.

How we work with you

Send us the draft or describe the deal on WhatsApp. We return a marked-up version with plain-language risk notes, join negotiation calls where useful, and finalize a signature-ready document.

When a contract includes an arbitration clause or needs judicial escalation, we coordinate directly with our arbitration or litigation team without needing to bring in outside counsel.
