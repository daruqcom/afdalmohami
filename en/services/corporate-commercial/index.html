Corporate & Commercial Legal Services

Corporate & Commercial legal services in Saudi Arabia.

Afdal Mohami's Corporate & Commercial practice covers the full range of legal services a company needs at every stage of its life, from initial formation through merger or acquisition, delivered to one service standard across every city in our network.

Whether you're an entrepreneur forming your first company, an executive at an established company needing a governance review, or a foreign investor exploring entry into the Saudi market, our Corporate & Commercial practice is designed to cover your legal needs across the company's entire lifecycle.

Key services within this practice area

This department includes company formation, often the starting point for any entrepreneur, alongside foreign investment and MISA licensing for investors coming from outside the Kingdom. We also help established companies with corporate governance and compliance to ensure their internal structures align with Saudi regulations, and with mergers and acquisitions when expanding or exiting the market. For those needing to reorganize their business structure, we offer business structuring tailored to their specific situation.

Who actually needs these services

This practice area typically serves entrepreneurs at the formation stage, family businesses planning to expand or bring in new partners, foreign investors seeking precise understanding of licensing requirements, and established companies facing regulatory scrutiny or planning an acquisition. Each of these situations follows a completely different legal path, and we determine the right path for you after understanding your actual situation in the first consultation.

How working with us works

Contact always starts with a short WhatsApp message describing your situation or project, followed by an assessment from a lawyer specializing in this exact practice area within minutes, then a clear plan defining timeline and cost before any formal commitment. In simple formation cases, basic procedures may be completed within a few days; complex mergers and acquisitions need a longer timeline covering due diligence and negotiation stages.

Examples of cases we handle

Recurring scenarios include an entrepreneur needing to quickly form a limited liability company before signing a contract with a major client, or a family business facing a dispute between partners over equity distribution when bringing in a new partner. Another common scenario: a foreign investor needing to understand the difference between various MISA license types before choosing the right legal form for their activity in Saudi Arabia.

Why choose Afdal Mohami for corporate and commercial matters

We combine deep practical experience in company formation and structuring across various sectors with up-to-date knowledge of constantly evolving foreign investment regulations, backed by a network covering 24 Saudi cities to one service standard. We make a point of explaining every legal option in language an entrepreneur understands, not complex legal jargon.

Quick Consultation vs. Extended Representation in Corporate Matters

Many straightforward formation and governance questions are resolved in a single consultation clarifying the correct route, while complex mergers, acquisitions, and shareholder disputes need extended representation spanning multiple parties and negotiation stages. We always help you identify which level actually fits your matter's scale, without charging for full representation you don't need.

Documents That Speed Up Any File in This Practice Area

For formation requests, having the partners' basic details and a clear description of the target commercial activity helps. For shareholder disputes and governance matters, having the incorporation contract, bylaws, and prior meeting minutes if available helps. For M&A transactions, recent financial statements and the target company's existing contracts significantly speed up the due diligence phase.

Sectors We Serve Within Corporate & Commercial

We handle files across diverse sectors including technology and telecom, retail and consumer, and healthcare, where licensing and governance requirements differ from one sector to another, and we apply this sector-specific understanding in every consultation we provide.

Questions Founders Ask Before Starting Any Procedure

Many entrepreneurs ask: should I start with a limited liability company or a sole establishment? The answer depends on the expected number of partners and the scale of risk you want to shield your personal assets from. Another common question: when do I actually need a written shareholder agreement instead of relying on trust between partners? The answer is always: from day one, since most shareholder disputes we see arise from the absence of a clear written agreement from the start.

How We Determine Fees for This Practice Area

We set fees based on the nature of the service requested: fixed fees for defined services like formation or drafting a specific contract, and hourly fees or a percentage of deal value for extended work like mergers and acquisitions. We clarify the expected cost with full transparency before starting any formal work, with no later surprises on the invoice.

Common Mistakes We Help You Avoid

Among the most common mistakes we see: forming a company through a verbal partnership without documenting each partner's shares and responsibilities, which later becomes a hard-to-resolve dispute at the first disagreement. Another common mistake: a foreign investor signing an investment agreement before confirming it matches the MISA licence type required for their activity, potentially requiring restructuring the whole deal later. We help you avoid these mistakes from the start through proactive review of every step.

Our Commitment to Corporate & Commercial Clients

We commit to giving a candid legal opinion even if it's not what you want to hear, since consultation credibility matters more than satisfying the client with a comfortable answer that could cost them later. We review every proposed contract or structure from an angle protecting your long-term interests, not just closing the current deal.

Regional Expansion and Handling Multiple Branches

Many companies operating in more than one city ask: do we need a different legal team for each city? No, our unified network covers 24 cities with the same service standard, meaning you deal with one team that understands your full situation instead of coordinating with multiple offices in each city you operate in. This unified follow-up becomes critical especially in group governance files for companies with branches or facilities across multiple regions, needing a unified policy that accounts for minor local municipal or regulatory differences without conflicting with overall legal guidance.

Proactive Legal Planning Instead of Reaction

We always encourage our clients to think of the legal side as part of strategic planning from the start, not a procedure resorted to only when a problem arises. Annual review of existing governance and contracts, and tracking any regulatory updates that may affect your business, significantly reduces the likelihood of entering costly disputes later.
