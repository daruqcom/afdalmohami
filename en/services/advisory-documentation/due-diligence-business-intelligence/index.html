Due Diligence & Business Intelligence Services in Saudi Arabia

Expert due diligence & business intelligence lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We conduct legal due diligence reviews before investment, acquisition, or partnership, to uncover hidden risks before any financial commitment is signed.

Before buying a stake in a company or entering a strategic partnership, we review the other party's complete legal standing: licences, existing contracts, open and potential disputes, and obligations toward employees and government entities, to uncover any hidden burden before it becomes yours.

We deliver a clear report prioritizing the risks, distinguishing what can be fixed before closing from what must be priced into the deal or grounds for withdrawal, and support the negotiating team with precise questions directed at the other party.

When you need this service

If you are about to invest in a company, acquire an existing business, or enter a major commercial partnership, due diligence before signing is far cheaper than discovering a problem afterward.

How we work with you

We define the review scope based on the nature of the deal, request and analyze documents against a comprehensive checklist, and deliver a risk report with our recommendations before the closing date.
