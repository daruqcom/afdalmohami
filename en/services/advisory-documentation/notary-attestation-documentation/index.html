Notary & Attestation & Documentation Services in Saudi Arabia

Expert notary & attestation & documentation lawyers in Saudi Arabia. Legal advice and court representation. Contact us today.

We handle notarization, notary services, and official attestation procedures for your documents - from powers of attorney to certifying contracts and foreign documents.

Proper notarization turns a verbal agreement or private document into a directly enforceable instrument. We obtain powers of attorney of all types through the notary services and the Najiz platform, and notarize contracts and acknowledgments in a way that gives them full legal standing.

We also handle attestation of foreign documents for use in the Kingdom through the multilateral apostille route or consular attestation, and arrange notarization of major commercial contracts with the notary office when the law requires it.

When you need this service

If you need an urgent power of attorney, a foreign document requiring attestation for official use in the Kingdom, or a commercial contract the law requires to be notarized, getting the procedure right the first time saves significant time.

How we work with you

Tell us the type of document and its purpose. We prepare the correct wording and process it through Najiz or directly with the notary office, and follow through until you receive the final notarized document.
