How to Claim Traffic Accident Compensation in Saudi Arabia

This guide explains How to Claim Traffic Accident Compensation in Saudi Arabia in plain language, with the practical steps anyone facing this situation in Saudi Arabia needs to know.

This guide explains the steps for claiming traffic accident compensation in Saudi Arabia, from documenting the accident and the traffic report to negotiating with insurance or escalating judicially.

The traffic report is the foundation of any compensation claim for a traffic accident, as it determines the fault percentage between involved parties, and this percentage directly affects the compensation amount due. Understanding how this report works and handling it correctly determines the claim's success.

Immediate Steps After an Accident

Clear photos of the accident scene and damages should be taken before moving vehicles if it can be done safely, and the competent authorities contacted to officially document the incident. If there are injuries, go immediately to the hospital and request a documented medical report of injuries and their occurrence date.

Understanding the Traffic Report and Fault Percentage

The traffic report determines each party's responsibility percentage for the accident based on site inspection and statements from parties and witnesses. This percentage is decisive: if you're 100% responsible you won't be entitled to compensation from the other party, while if responsibility is shared, your due compensation is reduced by your fault percentage.

How to Contest an Inaccurate Fault Percentage

If you feel the report didn't accurately reflect the incident, you can request a technical review of the report or submit additional evidence like nearby surveillance camera footage or additional witness testimony not taken at the time of initial drafting. This objection has a specific deadline that must be observed.

Claiming from the Insurance Company

The claim is first submitted to the insurance company attaching the traffic report, medical reports, and material damage invoices. Insurance companies are statutorily obligated to respond within a defined period, and any unjustified delay can be reported directly to the Insurance Authority as an effective pressure tool.

Compensation Elements Due

Compensation includes: vehicle repair costs or its actual value if a total loss, current and future medical treatment costs, lost earnings during the recovery period, diyah or arsh in death or severe injury cases, plus additional compensation for moral damage in serious cases.

What If the Insurance Company Refuses Fair Compensation?

When the insurance company falls short of offering compensation reflecting actual damage, it can be escalated to the competent insurance dispute committee, or a direct judicial claim filed against the at-fault party if insurance coverage is insufficient to cover the full damages.

When You Need a Specialized Traffic Accident Lawyer

The more severe the injuries or the more complex the fault percentage between parties, the greater the importance of specialized legal representation to ensure you receive the full compensation you deserve, not the low initial settlement insurance companies often offer.

Documents Needed to Claim Compensation

Claiming needs: the official traffic report, documented medical reports for injuries if any, vehicle repair invoices or estimates, and the insurance policy. No additional documents are needed at the initial consultation stage.

Common Question: How Long Does an Insurance Claim Settlement Usually Take?

Insurance companies are obligated to respond within a statutorily defined period, and simple claims may be resolved within weeks, while complex cases or those disputing the fault percentage need longer, potentially reaching months.

Conclusion: Immediate Documentation Is the Key to Fair Compensation

Speed in documenting the accident and the traffic report's accuracy are the decisive factors in any successful compensation claim. Don't hesitate to request technical review of any inaccurate report, and don't accept an insurance settlement below your actual right without legal consultation.

Practical Case: An Accident with Shared Responsibility

Imagine an accident where the traffic report determined 70% responsibility on the other driver and 30% on you. In this case, your due compensation is reduced by 30% (your fault percentage), so if your total damages are estimated at 100,000 riyals, you're actually entitled to only 70,000 riyals. Understanding this calculation is very important before accepting or rejecting any settlement offer from the insurance company.

Accidents Involving Rental Cars or Ride-Sharing Apps

If the accident occurred while using a rental vehicle or during a trip through a ride-sharing app, responsibility sometimes distributes between the driver themselves and the rental company or app depending on contract terms and the insurance policy linked to the vehicle, adding a potential party to claim against alongside the direct driver.

What If the Other Driver Is Uninsured?

If the at-fault party lacks valid insurance, recourse can be had to the fund protecting those affected by uninsured vehicle accidents, which provides alternative coverage in these cases, with the possibility of recovering from the violating driver directly later to collect any difference.

Fatal Traffic Accidents: The Rights of the Deceased's Heirs

If a party to the accident dies, the right to claim diyah and compensation passes to the legal heirs. Diyah is determined under its Sharia-prescribed amounts based on the type of death (accidental or quasi-intentional), with any additional civil compensation added for material damages suffered by the family, such as loss of the deceased breadwinner's income.

The Role of Witnesses in Strengthening Your Position

Testimony from witnesses present at the time of the accident adds real weight to your file, especially in cases disputing the fault percentage. Make sure to record the contact information of any witness present immediately after the accident occurs, as reaching them later may become difficult once parties disperse.

Acting quickly and documenting thoroughly gives you the strongest possible position when negotiating any settlement.
