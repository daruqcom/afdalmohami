Al-Baha's Distinct Economic Character

Al-Baha region is known for its temperate mountain climate and dense forest cover, making it a prominent summer tourism destination and a distinctive agricultural center for grapes, pomegranates, and mountain honey. This dual economic character between tourism and mountain agriculture creates a particular type of case: chalet and resthouse rental disputes during the summer season, and ownership of mountain agricultural land often distributed across small scattered plots among multiple heirs.

The region has also seen growth in mountain tourism projects and small resorts, requiring careful review of seasonal lease agreements and tourism activity licences with the Saudi Tourism Authority and the local municipality.
