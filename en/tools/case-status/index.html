Case Status & POA Lookup

Case Status & POA Lookup — free tool for Saudi Arabia.

This page explains how to check the status of an existing case or power of attorney document through the official channels available in Saudi Arabia.

Any party to an ongoing case can track its status electronically without needing to visit the court in person, through the Najiz platform which provides direct updates on the case status, upcoming hearing dates, and decisions issued.

How to Use This Tool

Log into the Najiz platform with your national ID number and password or through the unified national access, then go to the cases section to view all cases linked to your account with each case's details and current status.

This Tool's Limitations

Full access to a specific case's details requires you to be an official party to it (plaintiff, defendant, or documented representative); you cannot inquire about other parties' cases without an official capacity in them.

How to Check a Case or Power-of-Attorney Status

  • Official case status and power-of-attorney lookups are done through the Ministry of Justice's Najiz platform.
  • You'll need your national ID and case or deed number to look it up directly.
  • If you're an existing client, we can track your case status on your behalf and notify you of updates on WhatsApp.
